## The brief

Juniper's founders distil an aperitif from juniper, sorrel and garden herbs foraged outside Oslo. It tastes like nothing else and, in a dark bottle with a long label, it looked like everything else on the back bar.

## The idea

The drink is the colour. We made the green itself the brand: a clear bottle, one word on the front, and every image shot in hard light so the liquid glows and throws its colour across the table.

## What we made

A clear, heavy-based bottle and a quiet serif wordmark, a shoot system built on light and shadow, outer cartons, a bar kit for restaurants, and the e-commerce imagery for the launch in eleven countries.
